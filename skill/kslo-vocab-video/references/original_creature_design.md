## Start every prompt with the artwork-only declaration

> Pure illustration artwork only — NO card frame, NO border, NO HP text, NO game stats, NO logos, NO UI elements of any kind, NO speech bubbles, NO text of any kind anywhere in the image. Just the scene art filling the entire canvas edge to edge.

Adapt to a full-bleed **16:9** scene, preferably 2560×1440, and use the current image-tool schema (`gpt-image-2.5` when available). Do not copy the card skill's obsolete model name, 4:5 canvas, rarity-dependent placement, HP calculation or card UI.

## Preserve the video design requirements

1. Retain a central readable human participant and a prominent **ORIGINAL animal-inspired fantasy co-star** supporting the precise definition/sample-sentence meaning through action, interaction or expression. Do not turn the picture into a creature-only portrait or append a tiny passive pet.
2. Use an expressive anime face, memorable ears/tail, a coherent animal-based body plan and simple graphic markings. Vary genuine species/silhouette/anatomy/fantasy motif across the set, not only colour or accessories. Use accepted Unit 1 images as style/balance references, never as identities to recolour.
3. Give both characters coherent contact with props and environment. Use a detailed, integrated setting with vibrant colours, dynamic but readable lighting, confident outlines and smooth cel shading. Avoid pasted-on characters, generic plush/fleece/fuzzy baby-animal rendering, abstract blobs and realistic scaly lizards.
4. Keep faces, meaningful hands/paws, full co-star silhouette and essential props safe through the final centred 6% zoom. Check source images and actual 1100×619 video artwork cards; card-tier placement rules do not apply.
5. Forbid letters, digits, logos, price signs, labelled charts, UI, arrows, ticks/crosses, mathematical/music notation and floating explanatory symbols. Depict meaning using body language, action and concrete props, not speech bubbles or pictograms. Plain product packaging avoids fake brands.
6. Do not reproduce actual Pokémon or any other franchise identity, recognisable lookalike, creature-catching ball or trademark accessory. “Pocket-monster-style” is a design analogy for a distinct original fantasy animal, not permission to copy protected characters.
7. Check coherent bird beaks/mouths, all intended limbs, hands/grips, ordinary-device fronts/backs, clothing/waterline logic and meaningful scene variety. A fantasy accent does not excuse malformed anatomy or impossible devices.
